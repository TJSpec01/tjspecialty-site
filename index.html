About TJ Specialty

Experience, dedication and integrity

TJ Specialty Construction has been family-owned and operated since 1985, when Jim Kleinschmidt started the company with his wife, Sherry, and son, Kevin. What began as a small home-based business has grown into a thriving company with several dedicated and talented employees.

In our experience, construction is made up of two processes: first, building your project, and secondly, building relationships during the project. Our dedication to client satisfaction — and to projects completed on schedule and on budget — explains why so many new customers come from referrals.
